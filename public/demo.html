Scope: one niche, done well

This pilot only handles end of employment disputes in Brazil. Every story is routed to one of four lawyer style playbooks. Anything else is politely turned away instead of guessed at.

1. The worker tells their story

In the real product this is the only thing the user does: describe what happened in their own words. The examples below just fill the box for the demo. You can edit them or write a new story.

About 30 to 60 seconds. Four real model calls.

2. Triage: route, redact, pre check

A small, cheap model reads the story once: which playbook it belongs to and the key facts. Then code runs the checks that should never depend on a model.

3. Retrieved sources

The playbook's mandatory sources always come first, then BM25 keyword retrieval adds related provisions. Only these reach the drafting models.

4. Two models fill the playbook

Same playbook, same sources, different models. The models do not invent the letter's structure: they fill a lawyer approved skeleton with the worker's facts.

5. Guardrails, checked by code

No AI here. Hard failures block sending; soft ones lower the score.

6. Model C judges both drafts, blind

The judge does not know which model wrote which draft, and the order is shuffled to avoid position bias.

7. Score, gate and claim value

Overall = 50% citation accuracy + 30% citation strength + 20% letter quality, minus 15 for overreach. Any hard guardrail failure blocks sending.

Why Brazil, and what has to change

The UK model works partly because sending legal letters and advising on rights are not reserved activities. Brazil is different, so the product has to adapt before it can launch.

The constraint

The OAB statute makes legal consultancy and advice an activity exclusive to lawyers (Lei 8.906/94, art. 1º, II). A pure "no lawyers" product that advises workers would face regulatory risk.

The opening

In labour courts, workers can file and follow their own claims without a lawyer (jus postulandi, CLT art. 791), at first instance and regional courts (TST Súmula 425). So a tool that helps a worker organise facts, check the law and act in their own name has a real foothold.

Proposed model for a Brazil pilot

  • Self serve layer: the worker builds their case, sees the relevant law with verified citations, and sends a notice in their own name.
  • Partner lawyer layer: anything that is advice, or any case the gate sends to review, goes to an OAB registered partner lawyer who reviews and signs.
  • Business model to validate with counsel: fee structures between a tech company and lawyers have specific ethics rules in Brazil, so this needs legal review before launch.
  • Pilot scope: 3 claim types with clear statutory rules (unpaid overtime, late severance, pregnancy dismissal), one state.

Same workflow, UK version

Nothing in the pipeline is Brazil specific except the corpus and the value formulas. For the UK, swap in the Employment Rights Act provisions (including the 6 month qualifying period from January 2027), Acas early conciliation and tribunal time limits, and keep the verifier, judge, gate and funnel as they are.

Could work

  • Lawyer in the loop: Grapple's intake, triage, drafting and quality gate, with a partner labour lawyer who advises and signs. Mirrors Grapple's own structure inside Monaco Solicitors.
  • B2B tool for labour law firms: with 3.6 million new cases a year, productivity for lawyers is a big market and avoids the OAB conflict.
  • Free information and calculators (rights, deadlines, claim value) as top of funnel, handing off to lawyers.

Could NOT work

  • Copying the UK "no lawyers" model: legal advice is reserved for lawyers, and the OAB is actively suing.
  • "AI lawyer" marketing: fails with the OAB and, as DoNotPay showed, with consumer regulators.
  • A pure price pitch: many labour lawyers already charge nothing upfront and take a share of the result. (Assumption to validate.)

Unproven: test before scaling

  • Do letters settle? Many employers may wait for the court conciliation hearing instead. Measure reply and settlement rate per letter in a small pilot.
  • Cost of losing for workers: a losing worker can be ordered to pay the other side's legal fees (5% to 15%, CLT art. 791-A). For workers on free legal aid, the STF suspended this for 2 years (ADI 5766), so the risk is lower but real. The quality gate protects users, not only the brand.
  • Success fee structure: fee arrangements between a tech company and lawyers have specific ethics rules. Needs legal review.

Verdict

The tech travels, the business model does not. Enter Brazil as the tool that makes labour lawyers faster and safer, not as their replacement: partner with a Brazilian labour law firm, start with the four end of employment playbooks in one state, and prove letter to settlement conversion before scaling.

How it is built

Every part runs in this page. The architecture maps one to one to what I would build in production.

Story→PII redaction (code)→Triage · Haiku: playbook + facts→Pre checks (code): scope, time limit, required facts→Retriever: mandatory + BM25→Model A · HaikuModel B · Sonnet→Guardrails + citation verifier (code)→Judge · Opus, blind→Score + gateValue calculator (code)→Send / lawyer review / refuse→Event log → funnel

Design decisions

  • RAG holds the law, playbooks hold the letters. Retrieval finds which rules apply. The letter structure comes from one playbook per case type, written and approved by a lawyer. Models fill playbooks; they do not invent them.
  • One niche first. Four end of employment playbooks, everything else turned away. Easier to test, to get right, and to explain to a regulator.
  • Stop early, stop cheap. Out of scope, time barred or missing facts are caught after one small model call, before the expensive ones.
  • Code before AI for facts. Whether a quote exists in a source is checked by string matching, not by a model. Models only judge things that need judgement.
  • Two models, one judge. Lets us pick the cheapest model that passes the gate, and spot regressions when prompts or models change.
  • Blind, shuffled judging to reduce self preference and position bias.
  • Refusal is a feature. Grapple's promise is that it will not send a weak letter. The gate makes that measurable.
  • Style guard. Drafts are told to avoid typical AI tells (like em dashes) that opposing lawyers already flag.

Corpus

How we would know the letters are right

  • A Brazilian labour lawyer writes each playbook and 20 gold standard letters per playbook.
  • Every lawyer edit during review is saved and turned into new test cases.
  • The judge is calibrated against lawyer scores; once agreement is high enough, it reviews alone and lawyers sample.
  • Weekly regression run: every prompt or model change is tested on the full set before release.
